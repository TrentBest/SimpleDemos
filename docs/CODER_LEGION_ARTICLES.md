## Editorial guardrails for future SimpleDemos references

SimpleDemos should link to published articles rather than reproduce their narrative. When a new article is considered, compare it with this index first and give it a distinct question or new evidence.

- The original fox-and-dog article already introduces decoupled state machines and the simulation concept.
- “The Quick Brown Fox 2: The Silent Hordes,” the optimization log, and the benchmarking articles already cover scaling/performance narratives. Any new performance claim must state the exact scenario, build, measurement method, and what is or is not included.
- “Engineering ‘The’ Loop,” “The Deterministic Engine,” and the Unity integration articles already discuss loops and deterministic state management. A new article should focus on new executable evidence rather than retelling the premise.
- Distinguish the compiled C# FSM_API demo from the browser-side illustrative playground. Do not imply the browser animation is driven by the compiled assembly unless that integration is actually implemented and verified.
- A diagram or animation is presentation, not proof by itself. The planned proof path is a deterministic fixed-tick C# scenario, structured trace records and assertions, then an SVG view that consumes those records.
- Do not imply FSM_COS, GUI.SVG, Renderer, Visualize, WebApp, or other layers are integrated into SimpleDemos unless a real tested integration demonstrates that boundary.
- Treat all read/comment/reaction counts as the supplied snapshot; they will age and should be refreshed only when the author provides new figures.
