What just happened?

The fox is walking toward the sleeping dog. If it notices the dog in time, it calculates a jump over the dog's position.

The jump uses an inverted parabola: y = -4 × height × t × (1 - t). At t = 0.5, the fox is at the apex, horizontally centered over the dog.
